# El apestado poseído

The first part of the Wekufe's fight (`arg-tsr`): a plague-dead soldier ([apestado](apestado.md)) with the
Wekufe in him. A concept, proposed to the user on 30 October 2026 and shown in `modelos.html` as
"Apestado poseído" (`src/three/poseido.ts`); not yet agreed, not in the game.

## How he looks
The apestado's own model and clips, drawn 125 (the apestado 100, the Wekufe 150):
- Nearly black all over, the uniform still to be made out; what shone green in him shines red.
- Two ram's horns of plain geometry held by his head bone, coming through his kepi, the right one broken.
- Two red eyes, drawn through his brow (his model has none to light), not seen when he looks away.
- Black smoke and tongues of black fire with the Wekufe's red dodge off his shoulders and back, for the green stench.

## Being possessed (`agonia` in the gallery)
The user's choice: the Wekufe's agony (Zombie Agonizing, 3.2 s) and a black cloud wrapping him. He starts as the apestado, green and 0.8 of his size; the cloud climbs him from the ground as he writhes, is thickest about halfway, when he is changed under it, and thins away by 0.9 of the clip. Tongues of black fire, a faint red within them, lick up through it (the user).

## His leap (`salto` in the gallery, `LEAP`)
Mixamo's Mutant Jump Attack (the user's, 3 November 2026; 115 frames), played as it is: he crouches, springs high (the capture's own height, scaled to his legs) and forward about his height, lands and brings both hands down on the ground at frame 51. A red ring fills on the ground where he will land until the blow; then it goes out from his hands, with red cracks, black dust and fire, and the screen is jolted. Before it, Standing 2H Magic Area Attack 01 with a jump made in code (rough: he landed sideways with no real crouch) and Hard Landing (he only crouched) were tried.

## His death (`muerte` in the gallery, `DEATH`)
His own clip, falling; a black cloud gathers on him and hides the change: under it his horns and eyes go, he dwindles to 0.8 and lies as a green dead apestado. The Wekufe leaves by his mouth, a rope of black smoke and souls of black fire with the red dodge, arcing off to the next body (`DEATH.next`, 1.9 of his height to one side; nothing stands there in the gallery yet). 4.2 s in all. His body stays where it fell till the end of the fight (the user).

## How he fights (the user, 30 October 2026; not yet in the game)
He keeps all the apestado's powers (his blow, retching, charge) and adds the leap. Not to be struck till the cloud has gone. Quicker than an apestado and with more life. Three in all: each time one dies the Wekufe changes to the next.

Still open: whether the three are alike. In the gallery his retching is still the apestado's green.
